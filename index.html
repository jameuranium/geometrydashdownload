What is Geometry Dash?

A rhythm platformer developed by RobTop Games. Players face a series of challenging obstacles.

It is very easy to get into. You control a basic geometric character (usually a cube) and jump over deadly spikes and avoid obstacles.

However, it is well known for being challenging to play, as you can easily fail the stage if you miss one hit.

There is an amazing community with custom levels. These levels have custom designs, fun gameplay and amazing music.

There are easy levels for beginners and levels that are extremely hard to play. No matter what type of player you are, you will find something new to play. It is a very exciting game that will challenge your timing, patience and skill.

Get ready to jump in!
